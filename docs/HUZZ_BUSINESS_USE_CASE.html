1. Executive Summary

Huzz is a digital-only mobile software business operated by Saud as an individual software publisher registered with the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) under NTN 3120282643905. The business develops, publishes, and operates a social discovery mobile application for adults aged 18 and above. Users can create profiles, discover members, match, chat privately, join public clubs, and use optional live session features.

Huzz does not sell physical products and does not use cash-on-delivery. All services are delivered electronically through the Android application and a limited companion website. Payment gateway integration is required to accept electronic payments from customers in Pakistan for optional premium digital features and subscriptions. Settlement will be received in the verified Allied Bank account listed above.

2. Detailed Business Description

2.1 Nature of business

Huzz operates in software publishing and digital services. The primary product is the Huzz Android application distributed through Google Play. Supporting systems include cloud authentication, database storage, messaging, moderation, and push notifications. The business is home-based and operated from Bahawalpur, Punjab, Pakistan.

2.2 Problem solved

Users need a mobile-first platform to meet people in a structured environment with reporting, blocking, and moderation tools. Huzz provides profile-based discovery, mutual matching, private messaging, and admin review of reported behaviour.

2.3 Revenue model

  • Free tier: account creation, profile setup, and limited app features.
  • Paid tier: premium visibility, enhanced filters, extra messaging privileges, or subscription access.
  • All revenue is digital. No shipping or physical fulfillment.
  • Expected average ticket size: PKR 500 to PKR 5,000.
  • Expected launch volume: fewer than 500 transactions per month.

5. Product and Service Delivery Model

5.1 Customer journey

  1. Customer discovers Huzz through Google Play, website, or referral.
  2. Customer installs the app or opens the web companion.
  3. Customer registers using email authentication.
  4. Customer completes onboarding and accepts terms and privacy policy.
  5. Customer uses free digital features within platform limits.
  6. Customer selects a premium digital package inside the app or website.
  7. Customer completes payment on the licensed payment gateway checkout page.
  8. Gateway sends payment result to Huzz through secure callback or API.
  9. Huzz activates the purchased digital entitlement on the account within seconds.
  10. Customer may contact support by email for billing or access issues.

5.3 No physical delivery

Huzz sells access to software features only. There are no parcels, couriers, warehouses, or pickup points. Disputes usually relate to duplicate payment, failed activation, or account access rather than non-delivery of physical goods.

Huzz requires a licensed payment gateway to accept electronic payments for premium digital services. The gateway will be used only for lawful digital commerce related to the Huzz application. Funds will settle to the declared Allied Bank account after gateway fees.

  3. Huzz verifies payment records and entitlement usage.
