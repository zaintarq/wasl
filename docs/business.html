Refund and cancellation policy

Huzz sells instant digital services. Refunds may be granted in the following cases:

  • Duplicate payment: full refund within 7 business days after verification.
  • Payment successful but service not activated: full refund or immediate activation within 7 business days.
  • Accidental purchase within 24 hours: refund may be approved if the digital service was not materially used.
  • Change of mind after use: no refund unless required by applicable law.
  • Account banned for abuse or fraud: no refund for unused paid period.

Refund requests: email zain.tariq@mail.com with registered email, transaction date, amount, reference ID, and reason. Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days.
