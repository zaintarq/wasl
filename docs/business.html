What we do & our business goal

Our goal is to provide a safe, moderated, mobile-first platform where adults can discover people, start conversations, and build connections with clear community rules, reporting tools, and admin moderation.

What Huzz offers users (free tier)

  • Create a profile with photos, bio, preferences, and location (city-level)
  • Browse and discover other members based on preferences
  • Like, pass, and match with other users
  • Private one-to-one messaging after match or permission rules
  • Join public community clubs and read/participate in group chat
  • Block and report users who violate community guidelines

What we sell (paid digital tier — our revenue)

  • Huzz Premium subscription (monthly): extra visibility, advanced filters, unlimited likes, read receipts, and priority support
  • Profile Boost (one-time): temporary increased profile visibility in discovery feed
  • Super Like / Direct Message packs (one-time): digital credits to message or highlight interest in another user
  • Club premium features (future): optional paid club badges or enhanced club tools
Important: All paid products are digital services delivered instantly inside the app. There is no physical product, no shipping, and no offline payment collection by the merchant.

How we accept payments

Huzz accepts customer payments only through licensed payment gateways (such as Rapid Gateway) integrated into our mobile application and official checkout flow. We do not collect cash, bank transfers directly from customers, or offline payments.

Payment methods we plan to accept (via gateway)

  • Debit / credit cards (Visa, Mastercard where supported)
  • Bank account / IBFT transfers through gateway
  • Mobile wallets (JazzCash, Easypaisa, and other gateway-supported wallets)
  • Raast and other gateway-enabled instant payment rails in Pakistan

Step-by-step payment flow for customers

  1. User opens the Huzz Android app and signs in to their account.
  2. User navigates to Premium, Boost, or credit pack inside the app.
  3. User selects a digital product (e.g. Huzz Premium monthly plan).
  4. App creates an order and redirects user to the secure payment gateway checkout page.
  5. User completes payment using card, wallet, or bank method on the gateway.
  6. Gateway sends payment confirmation (success/failure) to Huzz servers via secure API callback.
  7. On success, Huzz instantly activates Premium/Boost/credits on the user's account (within seconds).
  8. User sees confirmation inside the app. User may receive email receipt where enabled.
  9. Gateway settles collected funds to our registered Allied Bank merchant account after standard settlement cycle.
