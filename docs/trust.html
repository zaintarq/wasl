Features

What you get in Huzz

Discover & match

  • Swipe or grid browse of nearby profiles with photos, bio, and preferences
  • Filters for country and discovery settings
  • Mutual likes open private chat automatically
  • Direct message option when you want to skip the wait

Private chat

  • One-to-one messaging with read receipts and reactions
  • Voice notes, photo sharing, and reply threads
  • AI conversation starters and optional message toxicity checks
  • Block, report, and unmatch at any time

Clubs

  • Join public clubs or enter with an invite code
  • Group text and voice-room style spaces for community hangouts
  • Club owners and admins can moderate membership

Live

  • Short, spontaneous live video sessions with another signed-in user
  • In-session text chat alongside video
  • Skip or leave anytime — no obligation to stay

Mehram supervision (optional)

  • Girls in a conversation can invite a trusted Mehram via a secure link — no Mehram account required
  • View-only or reply permissions, revocable at any time
  • Real-time message visibility for accountable, respectful chatting

Trust & verification

  • 18+ age check — facial age verification via ZoiVera (on-device scan; only a signed pass/fail is stored)
  • Photo verification (optional) — separate from the age gate; earns a verified badge on your profile
  • Location used for matching and safety (city/region level)
Privacy

How we handle your data

  • 18+ only. Age verification is required before likes, matches, chat, live, and clubs. Age status is set server-side — you cannot fake it from the app.
  • On-device age scan. ZoiVera runs facial estimation in your browser. Photos and video frames are not uploaded to Huzz or ZoiVera servers for that check.
  • Minimal retention mindset. We store what we need to run the app: account, profile, messages, safety logs, and session metadata — not more than necessary.
  • You control connection. Block anyone instantly. Reports go to our moderation queue.
  • Mehram tokens expire. Supervision links are time-limited, revocable, and hashed on our servers.
  • Live sessions. Real-time video uses encrypted WebRTC (LiveKit). Sessions are short-lived by design.
  • No selling your data. We do not sell personal information to advertisers. Huzz has no ad network in the app.
  • Account deletion. Email us to request deletion of your account and associated data.

Safety

Community standards

  • Zero tolerance for minors, grooming, harassment, hate, scams, or non-consensual content
  • In-app report and block on profiles, chats, and live
  • Automated and human review for flagged messages where configured
  • Device and account bans for serious or repeat abuse
  • Meet in public, tell someone where you are, trust your instincts — we say this because we mean it
Transparency

What we are building toward

Huzz is open-source friendly and community-driven. We ship iteratively: age assurance, Mehram supervision, live video, clubs, and stronger moderation are active areas — not marketing promises on a slide deck.
