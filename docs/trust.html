Our safety principles

  • Adults only (18+) — age assurance before likes, matches, messaging, live, and clubs.
  • Accountability — Mehram supervision lets a trusted person join a conversation with clear labels.
  • User control — block, report, and revoke access are always available.
  • Moderation — reports reviewed by authorized staff; repeat offenders removed.
  • No selling your data — we do not sell personal information.
  • Transparency — policies below explain data use, retention, and your rights.
