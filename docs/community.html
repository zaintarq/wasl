Community Guidelines & Moderation

Last updated: August 2026 · Report issues: zain.tariq@mail.com

Huzz exists so adults can connect with intention, dignity, and accountability. These guidelines apply to all features: Discover, chat, Mehram supervision, Social, Clubs, Live Random, and Games.

Golden rule: Treat others the way you would want your sister, brother, or best friend to be treated on a dating app.

1. Who can use Huzz

  • 18+ only. Misrepresenting your age is grounds for immediate removal and may be reported to authorities.
  • Real identity. Use recent photos of yourself. Catfishing and impersonation are prohibited.
  • One account per person unless we explicitly authorize otherwise (e.g. staff tools).

2. Respect & conduct

Allowed

  • Polite, honest conversation about interests, values, and intentions
  • Declining matches or conversations without harassment
  • Inviting a Mehram for supervision when comfortable
  • Leaving Live Random or club rooms at any time
  • Blocking users who make you uncomfortable

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or repeated unwanted contact after someone blocks or declines you
  • Hate speech or slurs targeting race, religion, ethnicity, gender, disability, or nationality
  • Sexual harassment, unsolicited explicit images, or pressure for intimate content
  • Violence or encouragement of self-harm
  • Scams, fraud, sugar scams, crypto schemes, or asking for money/gift cards
  • Sharing private chats or live sessions publicly without consent
  • Promoting illegal drugs, weapons, or illegal services
  • Spam, mass messaging, or promotional bots

3. Photos & profile content

  • Profile photos must be of you and appropriate for a public dating profile.
  • No nudity, sexually explicit content, or minors in photos (including background).
  • No violent, graphic, or hateful imagery.
  • Do not display phone numbers, social handles, or payment details in photos meant to bypass moderation.

4. Messaging & Mehram supervision

  • Private chats are for conversation between matched users. Mehram supervisors are invited by the female participant and must respect view-only or reply permissions.
  • Mehram messages are clearly labeled so all parties know who is speaking.
  • Attempting to deceive a Mehram supervisor (e.g. pretending to be the sister) is prohibited.
  • Girls may revoke Mehram access at any time. Mehram users may leave, report, or block on behalf of the inviting user where the feature allows.

5. Clubs & Live Random

  • Club rooms must follow club rules and these guidelines. No hate, harassment, or illegal activity in voice or text.
  • Live Random is for brief, respectful interaction. No nudity, sexual activity on camera, or illegal behavior.
  • Do not record others without consent where law requires consent.
  • Report inappropriate live behavior immediately using in-app report tools or email.

6. Games

  • Games are for friendly play with people you matched or know through the Service.
  • No cheating tools, abusive chat in game lobbies, or using games to harass users who blocked you elsewhere.

7. How to report

  1. In-app: Use Block or Report on profiles, chats, live sessions, or clubs where available.
  2. Mehram: Supervisors can report users from the supervision screen.
  3. Email: Send details to zain.tariq@mail.com including screenshots, usernames, and what happened.
  4. Child safety: If a minor is involved, email saudbilal1999@gmail.com immediately and contact local authorities if anyone is in danger.

8. Moderation process

Authorized Huzz staff may review reports, account data, messages, and media where necessary to enforce policies and applicable law.

StageWhat we do
Report receivedLogged with timestamp, reporter, target, and category
TriagePriority for threats, minors, non-consensual imagery, and violence
ReviewStaff examine relevant content and account history
ActionWarning, feature restriction, content removal, temporary ban, or permanent ban
EscalationSerious illegal activity may be referred to law enforcement

9. Enforcement actions

  • Warning — first-time minor violations
  • Feature limit — restrict messaging, live, or clubs
  • Content removal — photos or messages removed
  • Suspension — temporary loss of access
  • Permanent ban — account and device identifiers blocked where applicable
  • Legal referral — CSAE, threats, fraud, or other serious crimes

We may act without warning for severe violations (e.g. child exploitation, credible threats, fraud).

10. Appeals

If you believe we made a mistake, email zain.tariq@mail.com from your registered address with your username and explanation. We will review in reasonable time but are not obligated to reinstate accounts that pose ongoing risk.

11. Offline safety

  • Meet in public for first meetings.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you are going.
  • Arrange your own transport.
  • Never send money to someone you have not met in person and verified.
  • Trust your instincts — leaving is always okay.
