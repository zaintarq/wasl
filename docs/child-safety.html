Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 2026 · Safety contact: saudbilal1999@gmail.com

Zero tolerance: Huzz prohibits child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, and any use of our platform to harm minors. Violations result in permanent bans and may be reported to authorities.

Huzz is an adults-only (18+) dating and social discovery application. We maintain zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Adults only

  • Huzz is intended exclusively for users aged 18 and older.
  • Age verification is required before core features unlock.
  • We do not knowingly permit minors to create accounts.
  • Accounts suspected of belonging to minors are removed after review.

2. In-app reporting and blocking

  • Users can report other users and concerning content from profiles, chats, live sessions, and clubs.
  • Users can block other users to prevent further contact.
  • Mehram supervisors can report concerning behavior from the supervision screen.
  • Child safety reports receive priority review.

3. Moderation and enforcement

  • Automated signals and human review are used to detect policy violations.
  • Accounts involved in CSAE are permanently banned.
  • Relevant information is preserved for investigations and legal compliance.

4. Prohibited conduct

  • CSAM or any sexual content involving minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or exploitation of minors
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing material that sexualizes minors
  • Using Huzz to locate, contact, or harm minors
  • Any conduct that facilitates CSAE

5. Reporting to authorities

We comply with applicable child safety laws. Where required, we report CSAM and related illegal activity to appropriate authorities (including organizations such as NCMEC in the United States, where applicable).
