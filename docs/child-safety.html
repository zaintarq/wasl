Huzz Child Safety Standards

Last updated: July 24, 2026 · Safety contact: saudbilal1999@gmail.com

Huzz is an adults-only (18+) dating and social discovery application. We maintain zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, and any attempt to use our platform to harm minors.

1. Adults only

  • Huzz is intended exclusively for users aged 18 and older.
  • We do not knowingly permit minors to create accounts or use the service.
  • Accounts suspected of belonging to minors are removed after review.

2. In-app reporting and blocking

  • Users can report other users and concerning content from within the app.
  • Users can block other users to prevent further contact.
  • Reports related to child safety receive priority review.

3. Moderation and enforcement

  • We use automated tools and human review to detect and act on policy violations.
  • We may suspend or permanently ban accounts involved in CSAE or related harm.
  • We preserve relevant information as required for safety investigations and legal compliance.

4. Prohibited conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on Huzz:

  • CSAM or any sexual content involving minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or exploitation of minors
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing material that sexualizes minors
  • Using Huzz to locate, contact, or harm minors
  • Any conduct that facilitates CSAE

5. Reporting to authorities

Huzz complies with applicable child safety laws. Where required by law, we report CSAM and related illegal activity to appropriate regional and national authorities, including relevant law enforcement or dedicated reporting organizations (such as NCMEC in the United States, where applicable).

7. User guidance

If you see content or behavior that may put a child at risk, report it immediately in the app and email saudbilal1999@gmail.com. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
