Adults only

  • Wasl is for users 18 and older
  • Age verification required before core features
  • We do not knowingly allow minors
  • Suspected minor accounts removed after review

Reporting & blocking

  • Report users and content from profiles, chats, live, clubs
  • Block to stop further contact
  • Mehram supervisors can report from the supervision screen
  • Automated moderation flags feed into admin review

Prohibited conduct

  • CSAM or sexual content involving minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or exploitation of minors
  • Sharing or requesting material that sexualizes minors
  • Using Wasl to locate, contact, or harm minors
  • Any conduct that facilitates CSAE

Law enforcement

We comply with child safety laws. CSAM and related illegal activity may be reported to authorities (including NCMEC in the US where applicable).

Relevant data is preserved for investigations.
