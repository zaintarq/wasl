Huzz is an adults-only (18+) social connection app. Connect freely with people near you. This listing describes exactly what you can do inside the app.

WHO IT IS FOR
Huzz is for adults who want to meet new people, connect with others, chat privately, and optionally join short live video sessions. It is not for children.

CORE FEATURES

1) DISCOVER PROFILES
• Scroll a home feed of user profile cards
• See photos, name, age, city/region, and bio
• Filter who you see by preferences such as location and intent

2) LIKE, CONNECT & MESSAGE
• Like someone to show interest
• Message someone directly from their profile
• When you both like each other, a private chat opens automatically

3) PRIVATE CHAT
• Send text messages to your connections
• Send voice notes
• Block or report a user from chat options
• Optional translation for received messages

4) LIVE RANDOM (OPTIONAL)
• Separate section for spontaneous live sessions with other signed-in users
• Real-time text chat during the session
• Optional camera and microphone when you choose to enable them
• Skip or leave anytime; sessions are short and opt-in

5) YOUR PROFILE & SETTINGS
• Add and edit profile photos and bio
• Manage notification preferences
• Sign in with email/password or Google

SAFETY
• 18+ only
• In-app block and report
• Moderation and review of safety reports

PERMISSIONS (WHY WE ASK)
• Location — show your city/region for matching
• Camera & microphone — only for Live Random when you turn them on
• Photos — add profile pictures and share images in chat
• Contacts — safety features on device
