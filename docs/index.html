About Huzz

Huzz helps you meet people nearby and connect when the interest is mutual. Browse profiles, refine who you see with filters, and start private chats with your matches — including voice notes, translation, and AI conversation starters.

Beyond one-to-one chat, Huzz offers clubs for group text and voice rooms, and Live for short, spontaneous sessions with new people. Sign up on the web or Android — your account works across both.

Stay safe — meet in public, trust your gut, block or report from any profile or chat.
