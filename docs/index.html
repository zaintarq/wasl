Connection with intention

Huzz is for adults who want to meet in a halal, respectful way — with accountability built into the experience.

  • 🛡️
    Mehram supervision Sisters can invite a trusted Mehram to view or join a private chat — no extra account needed.
  • 💬
    Match & chat Discover nearby profiles, match with intention, and talk privately with voice notes and translation.
  • 📹
    Live Random Short, timed live video sessions with another real signed-in user — skip or leave anytime.
  • 👥
    Clubs Join community spaces for group chat and voice rooms — hang out beyond one-to-one.
  • ✓
    18+ verified Age assurance before likes, matches, chat, live, and clubs — adults only, always.
  • ⚡
    Safety first Block, report, and unmatch instantly. Meet in public and keep your wali or Mehram in the loop.

Stay safe — meet in public, trust your gut, and block or report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
