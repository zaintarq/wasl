What is Huzz?

Huzz is a mobile social app for discovery, mutual connections, private chats, voice clubs, and live sessions. Browse profiles, connect when interest is mutual, and join club spaces to chat and hang out with your community.

Discover Browse and connect with people near you
Chats Message after a mutual connection
Clubs Join public or private spaces with voice chat
Live Short random live sessions when you want spontaneity
