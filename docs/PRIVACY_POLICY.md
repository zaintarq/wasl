# Huzz Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 7, 2026

**Contact:** support@huzz.com

Huzz (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the Huzz mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices.

By using Huzz, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Who we are

Huzz is a social connection app that lets users browse profiles, connect with other users, chat, and optionally join short live video/text sessions with other signed-in users.

**Developer contact:** support@huzz.com

---

## 2. Information we collect

### Account and profile information
- Email address and authentication credentials
- Name, age, gender, bio, interests, and profile photos
- Discovery preferences such as location and discovery filters
- Optional voice profile audio

### Location information
- Approximate location (city and country/region) for discovery and safety
- We do not sell precise GPS coordinates to other users as a default product feature

### Contacts (required to use the app)
- With your permission, we access and upload your device contacts (names, phone numbers, email addresses, and related contact fields) to our servers
- Contact data is used for safety features, including blocking people you may know, and for operational review by authorized staff where permitted by law
- You must grant contacts permission to use the app

### Photos and media
- Profile photos and images you choose to upload
- Photo library access is required to add profile photos and share images in chat

### Messages and interactions
- Text messages, voice notes, reactions, and metadata (timestamps, read status)
- Likes, passes, connections, blocks, and reports
- Live Random session messages and session metadata (duration, participants)

### Live video and audio
- If you use Live Random video chat, camera and microphone data are transmitted in real time through our video infrastructure (LiveKit) to your session partner
- We do not record live video sessions by default unless we explicitly tell you otherwise in the app

### Device and technical data
- Device identifiers, operating system, app version
- Push notification tokens
- IP address and network-related signals (including for abuse and VPN detection)
- Crash and performance logs

### Safety and moderation
- Reports you submit, moderation flags, and safety event logs
- Automated scans of message text for policy violations

---

## 3. How we use information

We use information to:
- Create and manage your account
- Show you other users and apply your filters and preferences
- Enable discovery, messaging, and Live Random sessions
- Send push notifications (chats, messages, alerts)
- Keep users safe through blocking, reporting, moderation, and admin review
- Prevent fraud, spam, abuse, and policy violations
- Improve, maintain, and secure the App
- Comply with legal obligations

---

## 4. How we share information

We may share information with:
- **Other users:** Profile information you choose to show, and messages/media you send to connections or live session partners
- **Service providers:** Cloud hosting (Google Firebase), push notifications (Expo), live video (LiveKit), email delivery, and analytics/security tools that help us run the App
- **Moderators and admins:** Authorized staff may access account, contact, device, and safety data to investigate reports and enforce policies
- **Legal requests:** When required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

We do not sell your personal information.

---

## 5. Data retention

We retain information while your account is active and as needed for safety, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement. You may request account deletion by contacting support@huzz.com.

---

## 6. Your choices

- **Permissions:** You can change camera, microphone, location, contacts, and photo permissions in device settings, but some permissions are required to use core features
- **Blocking and reporting:** You can block users and report inappropriate behavior in the app
- **Notifications:** Manage notification preferences in app settings where available
- **Account deletion:** Email support@huzz.com to request deletion
