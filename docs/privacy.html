Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Contact: zain.tariq@mail.com

Huzz ("we", "us", "our") operates the Huzz mobile application and website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices.

By using Huzz, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

Huzz is a dating and social discovery platform for adults 18+. Users browse profiles, match, chat, join clubs, use Live Random, play games, and optionally invite Mehram supervisors into conversations.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile

  • Email address and authentication credentials
  • Name, username, age, gender, bio, interests, religion, and preferences
  • Profile photos and optional voice profile audio

Location

  • Approximate location (city and country) for matching and safety

Contacts

  • With your permission, device contacts may be accessed for safety features as described in-app

Messages and activity

  • Chat messages, voice notes, reactions, matches, likes, blocks, and reports
  • Mehram supervision metadata (invite status and permissions; Mehram users do not get a full dating profile)
  • Club membership, live session messages, and game session metadata
  • Message moderation metadata when automated scans flag content (categories, matched terms, scores)

Live video and audio

  • Live Random transmits camera and microphone in real time to session partners via LiveKit
  • We do not record live video by default unless explicitly stated in-app

Age verification (ZoiVera)

  • Facial age estimation runs on your device through our partner ZoiVera
  • We receive and store a cryptographic attestation result (pass/fail, provider, method, timestamp). We do not store a gallery of raw biometric face images for dating features

Device and safety data

  • Device identifiers, a device fingerprint hash used for fraud and device-ban enforcement, OS, app version, IP address, crash logs, and push notification tokens
  • Blocked profanity attempts, safety event logs, and aggregated user safety profiles used for moderation

3. How we use information

  • Provide discovery, matching, messaging, clubs, live, games, and Mehram features
  • Verify age with ZoiVera and prevent underage access
  • Run automated moderation (profanity blocking, keyword scans, NSFW photo detection)
  • Investigate reports, enforce blocks, and apply device bans when necessary
  • Send notifications you opt into
  • Improve and secure the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How we share information

  • Other users: Profile fields you choose to show; messages you send to matches, club members, or live partners
  • Mehram supervisors: Access only to the invited conversation, per permission granted
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (hosting, auth, database, storage), Expo (push notifications), LiveKit (live video), ZoiVera (age verification)
  • Moderators: Authorized staff may access account, message, report, and device data to investigate safety issues
  • Legal: When required by law or to protect safety

We do not sell your personal information.

Read our full Safety & Moderation policy for how we handle sexting prevention, reporting, blocking, Mehram supervision, and device bans.

We retain data while your account is active and as needed for safety, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Request deletion at zain.tariq@mail.com.

7. Your choices

  • Block and report users in-app
  • Email us to access, correct, or delete your data where applicable law provides rights
