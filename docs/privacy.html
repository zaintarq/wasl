Huzz Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Contact: zain.tariq@mail.com

Huzz (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the Huzz mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices.

By using Huzz, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

Huzz is a dating and social discovery app that lets users browse profiles, match with other users, chat, and optionally join short live video or text sessions with other signed-in users.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

  • Email address and authentication credentials
  • Name, age, gender, bio, interests, and profile photos
  • Relationship intent and discovery preferences
  • Optional voice profile audio

Location information

  • Approximate location (city and country/region) for matching and safety

Contacts (required to use the app)

  • With your permission, we access and upload your device contacts, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, and related contact fields, to our servers
  • Contact data is used for safety features and operational review by authorized staff where permitted by law

Photos and media

  • With your permission, we may upload photos from your device photo library to our servers
  • Profile photos and images you choose to share in chat

Messages and interactions

  • Text messages, voice notes, reactions, and metadata
  • Likes, passes, matches, blocks, and reports
  • Live Random session messages and session metadata

Live video and audio

  • If you use Live Random video chat, camera and microphone data are transmitted in real time to your session partner through our video infrastructure
  • We do not record live video sessions by default unless we explicitly tell you otherwise in the app

Device and technical data

  • Device identifiers, operating system, app version
  • Push notification tokens
  • IP address and network-related signals
  • Crash and performance logs

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show you other users and apply your filters and preferences
  • Enable matching, messaging, and Live Random sessions
  • Send push notifications
  • Keep users safe through blocking, reporting, moderation, and admin review
  • Prevent fraud, spam, abuse, and policy violations
  • Improve, maintain, and secure the App
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How we share information

  • Other users: Profile information you choose to show, and messages or media you send to matches or live session partners
  • Service providers: Cloud hosting (Google Firebase), push notifications (Expo), live video (LiveKit), and related infrastructure providers
  • Moderators and admins: Authorized staff may access account, contact, device, photo, and safety data to investigate reports and enforce policies
  • Legal requests: When required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention

We retain information while your account is active and as needed for safety, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement. You may request account deletion by contacting zain.tariq@mail.com.

6. Your choices

  • You can change camera, microphone, location, contacts, and photo permissions in device settings, but some permissions are required to use core features
  • You can block users and report inappropriate behavior in the app
  • You can manage notification preferences in app settings where available
  • Email zain.tariq@mail.com to request account deletion

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), authenticated cloud infrastructure, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children

Huzz is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. Contact us if you believe a minor has created an account.

9. International users

Your information may be processed in countries where we and our service providers operate.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the new version with an updated date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
